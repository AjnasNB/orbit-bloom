Privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026. Publisher: Ajnas N B.

Orbit Bloom stores garden progress, farm timers, lives, settings, and the local coin balance on your device. Guest play needs no account. You can optionally connect your Apple Game Center account and enable private iCloud game backups. There is no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or publisher-operated game server.

Optional Game Center and iCloud saves

Apple handles Game Center sign-in; the app does not receive your Apple password or request your email, contacts or friends list. Your Game Center nickname is displayed in the player screen. A hash of Apple's game-scoped player identifier separates local profiles and saved games. GameKit stores the complete game wallet, including progress, farm timers, coins, lives, tools, puzzle state, settings and credited purchase transaction identifiers, in your private iCloud Drive. Use the same Game Center and iCloud accounts on each device with iCloud Drive enabled.

Each device keeps its own cloud backup. When saved gardens differ, you choose which to continue. Balances are not combined. The app keeps a local checkpoint before a cloud restore and refuses a restore missing a purchase credited to the current garden. Backups require a connection and may be delayed; check the last successful backup before deleting or changing devices. You can pause automatic cloud backup in the player screen. Resetting local progress pauses backup and does not delete existing iCloud saves. Manage your Apple accounts and iCloud data through Apple's device settings.

The publisher does not receive your Game Center credentials, nickname, identifier or saved games. Cloud saves are not sent to the game website, Cloudflare or a publisher database. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment details. The app reads verified product transactions to grant purchased items and stores transaction identifiers locally to prevent duplicate grants. We do not receive your card number or banking details. Apple's own services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting the app can delete device-local progress and consumable balances. A successfully saved iCloud garden can be selected on another device using the same Apple game and iCloud accounts; an unsynced local change cannot be recovered from the cloud. Non-consumable ownership can be restored through Apple; consumable coins and lives are not restored after they have been consumed. Use the in-app Restore purchases action for eligible ownership.

No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or health permission is requested. Music and artwork are bundled for offline gameplay.

For support or privacy questions, open an issue in the project support tracker. Do not include payment credentials, receipts, personal identifiers, or other private information in a public issue.

This policy must be updated before adding publisher accounts, advertising, telemetry, or other data collection.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes ordinary web requests, including IP addresses and technical request information, to deliver and protect the site. This site has no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, account form or cookie banner. The game itself saves locally and, when enabled, through Apple's iCloud service; it does not send gameplay data to this website.
