Privacy policy

Effective 8 October 2026. Publisher: Ajnas N B.

Orbit Bloom stores garden progress, farm timers, lives, settings, and the local coin balance on your device. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or publisher-operated game server. We do not receive your saved game or use it for advertising.

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment details. The app reads verified product transactions to grant purchased items and stores transaction identifiers locally to prevent duplicate grants. We do not receive your card number or banking details. Apple's own services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting the app can delete device-local progress and consumable balances. Non-consumable ownership can be restored through Apple; consumable coins and lives are not restored after they have been consumed. Use the in-app Restore purchases action for eligible ownership.

No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or health permission is requested. Music and artwork are bundled for offline gameplay.

For support or privacy questions, open an issue in the project support tracker. Do not include payment credentials, receipts, personal identifiers, or other private information in a public issue.

This policy must be updated before adding cloud saves, accounts, advertising, telemetry, or other data collection.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes ordinary web requests, including IP addresses and technical request information, to deliver and protect the site. This site has no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, account form or cookie banner. The game itself stores progress locally.
