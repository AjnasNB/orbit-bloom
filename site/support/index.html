Support & how to play

Report a gameplay issue. Include your device model, iOS version, stage/activity, and steps to reproduce. Do not post payment credentials or private account details.

Explore your island

Swipe across the island map to visit 102 pages with 1,020 stages. Complete stages in order to unlock the next stop. Tap the farm or rover inside the scene to visit those activities. The farm, tool shed, field journal and supplies use swipe pages. Opening puzzles have 10–20 turns; later stages have 16–20.

How to play

Swipe neighboring pieces to match three in Bloom circuits, or tap connected groups of two or more. Bloom beside frost to melt it. Form power patterns, then tap a board power-up to trigger chain reactions. Select an inventory tool, then tap its target. Rainbow clears the target color.

Farm: choose roses or apples, plant an empty plot, water it, and harvest when ready. Compost crafts puzzle tools. Harvested cargo boosts race rewards. Race: swipe left or right on the road to steer.

Lives and purchases

Five normal puzzle lives regenerate one every 30 minutes. A win returns a life. Extra purchased lives are a separate reserve and do not expire. Farm and Race remain open while you wait. Earn coins there to refill normal lives.

Purchases use Apple's confirmation sheet and current local storefront price. An unavailable product stays disabled. Pending Ask to Buy purchases grant items only after approval. Restore purchases checks eligible non-consumable ownership; consumables are not restorable after use. Progress is device-local, so reinstalling may lose a save.

Sound

Settings contains separate music, sound, and haptic switches. Music respects the iPhone silent setting and pauses when the app is inactive.

Hints, shuffles and patterns

Your first 10 hints are free. Permanent field tasks earn more hints, shuffles and puzzle tools. After free supplies, a hint costs 3 coins and a shuffle costs 15 coins. Swipe neighboring pieces to make a line of three, or tap connected groups. Four in line creates a Bomb, L/T creates TNT, a seven-piece cross creates Mega, and five in line creates Rainbow. Tap a board power-up to trigger it; nearby power-ups chain together.
