01

Bloom Circuits

Swipe neighboring flowers and fruit. Form power patterns, clear frost, and set off chain reactions within a tight turn budget.

Four in a line creates a Bomb. L and T formations create TNT. Five in a line creates Rainbow. Earn hints and shuffles through field tasks.

02

Farm Terraces

Plant roses and apples, water your plots, and harvest coins, compost and cargo.

Craft stronger tools from compost. Complete permanent field tasks for extra TNT, hints and shuffles.

03

Harvest Rally

Swipe your delivery rover between three lanes. Dodge barriers, catch coins, and bring the harvest home.

A 22-second delivery run with five difficulty tiers as deliveries succeed. Farm cargo adds a delivery bonus.

04

Canal Weave

Rotate pipe pieces to connect the inlet to the outlet. Restore the water route feeding Lio’s terraces.

Logic challenges grow into larger boards. A new clear earns one star, 25 coins and two water.

05

Firefly Trail

Watch numbered lanterns light up, then repeat their sequence. Guide the atoll’s pollinators along the trail.

Longer sequences test your memory as you progress. A new clear earns one star, 25 coins and two water.

06

Windmill Works

Time each charge as the pointer enters the marked window. Power the workshop that helps Lio rebuild.

Charge windows tighten as you progress. VoiceOver offers guided timing. A new clear earns one star, 25 coins and two water.

07

Moon Observatory

Slide numbered star tiles into the gap to put the chart in order. Recalibrate the moonseed network after the Eclipse.

Each of the four skill rooms supports up to 1,000 procedural challenges. Completed clears and best scores save; unfinished attempts do not.

Space to breathe.

Explore a 3D island across 102 swipe pages and spend earned stars on six restoration projects. Enjoy dimensional botanical pieces, music and action sounds. All rooms can be played offline, without an account or advertising. Optional UTC world events reward harvests, deliveries and new skill-room clears; event bonuses require a recent online clock check. Puzzle lives are spent when a stage starts, stay spent if you abandon it, and return on a win. The other six rooms are life-free.
