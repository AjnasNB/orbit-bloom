# Living farm redesign — build 15

The user reported that the Farm room resembled boxes and that planting a tree produced a generic leaf. The previous room used six rounded cards with a single growing sprite. The new room is a continuous native SceneKit field with furrowed oval soil beds, crossing paths, a farmhouse, stream, bridge, borders and harvest basket. Six numbered action capsules are projected from the actual camera onto each bed; there is no scroll view or separate farm navigation bar.

Apple and rose crops now have distinct visible growth. Apples begin as a twig with leaves, then grow branches and canopy, blossom and bear seven red apples. Roses develop shoots, stems, buds and layered pink flowers. Both use the new pointed, veined leaf artwork. The field leaf planes face the camera so the blade is readable instead of showing thin edges. The puzzle also replaces its old leaf while retaining the other eleven sprite indices. The original transparent PNG and full generation prompt are recorded in [FARM_LEAF_PROMPT.json](FARM_LEAF_PROMPT.json).

Growth derives from each existing saved crop and `readyAt`; no saved fields or migration were added. Rose and apple harvest times remain 30 and 45 seconds. Water advances the deadline by ten seconds. Species selection affects only a new planting: an existing apple stays an apple when roses are selected. Returning after closing the app shows the elapsed growth, and harvesting clears that bed and grants the existing coins, compost and cargo once. Growth scaling, gentle breeze, water droplets and harvest movement provide visual feedback. Reduce Motion disables these actions. Existing farming, watering and harvesting sounds remain connected to successful actions.
