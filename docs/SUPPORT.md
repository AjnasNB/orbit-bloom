# Orbit Bloom support

[Report a gameplay issue](https://github.com/AjnasNB/orbit-bloom/issues). Include your device model, iOS version, stage/activity, and steps to reproduce. Do not post payment credentials or private account details.

## How to play

Swipe neighboring pieces to match three in Bloom circuits, or tap connected groups of two or more. Bloom beside frost to melt it. Four in a line makes a Bomb, L/T makes TNT, a seven-piece cross makes Mega, and five in line makes Rainbow. Tap a power on the board to trigger it and chain nearby powers. Select an inventory tool, then tap its target. Rainbow clears the target color.

Farm: choose roses or apples, plant an empty plot, water it, and harvest when ready. Compost crafts puzzle tools. Harvested cargo boosts race rewards. Race: swipe left or right on the road to steer.

## Lives and purchases

Five normal puzzle lives regenerate one every 30 minutes. A win returns a life. Extra purchased lives are a separate reserve and do not expire. Farm and Race remain open while you wait. Earn coins there to refill normal lives.

Purchases use Apple's confirmation sheet and current local storefront price. An unavailable product stays disabled. Pending Ask to Buy purchases grant items only after approval. Restore purchases checks eligible non-consumable ownership; consumables are not restorable after use. Progress is device-local, so reinstalling may lose a save.

## Sound

Settings contains separate music, sound, and haptic switches. Music respects the iPhone silent setting and pauses when the app is inactive.

## Hints, shuffles and tasks

First 10 hints and 3 shuffles are free. Later hints cost 3 earned coins and shuffles cost 15. Six permanent field tasks award tools and assistance after farming, delivery, circuit and blast milestones. Each reward can be claimed once. There are no daily quest deadlines.
