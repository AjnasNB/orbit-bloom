# Orbit Bloom product page artwork prompts

Both assets were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 9 October 2026. They are original brand illustrations, not gameplay screenshots. Generated files were technically exported with macOS `sips` to Apple's required dimensions, without creative alterations. The header's generated source was 1915 × 821 and the search source was 1536 × 1024; the upload files are 3840 × 1646 and 1920 × 1280 respectively, fully opaque. Native gameplay PNGs are copied byte-for-byte and are not resized.

## Header

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: Apple App Store product page HEADER for Orbit Bloom: Garden Arcade. Produce ONE wide panoramic original artwork, exact 3840 by 1646 pixels (21:9), fully opaque PNG. This is expressive brand artwork, NOT a screenshot and NOT a mock app interface. Scene: a beautifully lit botanical microcosm among the stars, luminous real-shaped emerald leaves with delicate veins, one coral rose, crisp red apple, a faceted purple diamond and crystal blue dewdrop gathered around a graceful glowing golden orbital arc. These are the five botanical pieces used by the actual game. Subtle friendly brass gardening rover and a compact dark teal round garden bomb with a short tiny warm fuse may be secondary accents. Composition: single strong focal point formed by the leaves, rose and orbit, held within the central 60% of the frame so it remains legible in Apple's crops. Outer sides are airy pale mint and warm ivory atmospheric space with sparse tiny golden stars. Premium stylized 3D render, tactile polished materials, soft depth and warm sunlight, clean joyful mobile garden-game brand illustration. Refined emerald, pale mint, ivory, coral and honey gold palette; keep saturation restrained beyond the botanical focal elements. No extra game modes, no humans, no guns, no dense landscape or false gameplay representation. No text, no lettering, no badges, no prices, no website URLs, no Apple/device/platform logos, no watermark, no transparency. Make the image elegant and readable when used as a short wide header.
